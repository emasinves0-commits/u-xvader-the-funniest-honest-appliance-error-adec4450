# Why this line

> Drain pump blocked by 147 unbroken chopsticks. Water level rising. Proceeding anyway.

The joke has three parts:

- **A real mechanism.** Utensils and small objects that escape the rack and jam the drain pump are a genuine dishwasher failure mode, so the error reads as plausible.
- **One specific number.** "147 unbroken chopsticks" is exact and a little absurd. The word "unbroken" adds to the picture: the machine tried to chew through them and failed.
- **A flat machine turn.** "Proceeding anyway." The appliance reports the problem accurately, then carries on, which is the honest-error joke.
